Summary: MITRA ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by Blackhole Infiverse LLP, provides AI companion, productivity, and messaging automation services. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with global standards, including Meta Platform Terms and WhatsApp Business Policies.

1. Information We Collect

When you use MITRA and its integrated communication capabilities (including WhatsApp Business, Email, Calendar, and Ecosystem tools), we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, business name, phone number, email address, and authentication credentials when linking services.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform Data: Phone Number ID, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID, message delivery status, recipient phone numbers, and message template metadata required for sending and receiving messages.
  • Communications & Interactions: Text queries, AI chat prompts, calendar scheduling events, and command preferences submitted through our companion interface.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, device metadata, browser type, interaction timestamps, and API latency logs to ensure system reliability and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly for the following authorized business and service purposes:

  • To provide and deliver automated AI companion services, conversational assistance, and workflow automation.
  • To transmit user-initiated WhatsApp notifications, reminders, and alerts via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • To authenticate and manage customer onboarding via Meta-hosted Embedded Signup.
  • To facilitate user-approved calendar synchronization, email drafting, and document analytics.
  • To maintain system integrity, detect and prevent fraud or security breaches, and comply with applicable laws.

3. Meta WhatsApp Cloud API Data Handling

MITRA operates as a Tech Provider leveraging Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. In accordance with Meta Platform Terms:

  • We process WhatsApp messages and customer phone numbers only on behalf of the customer and strictly according to their instructions.
  • We do not use customer message contents or WhatsApp contact numbers for advertising, profiling, or cross-service tracking.
  • All message transmission is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS protocols between MITRA servers and Meta Graph API endpoints.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. We only share data with trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Strictly to deliver WhatsApp Business messages, template management, and Embedded Signup authentication.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure hosting and database infrastructure operated in compliant data centers with enterprise-grade physical and digital security.
  • Legal Requirements: If compelled by applicable statutory law, court subpoena, or authorized law enforcement requests.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide active companion services or fulfill statutory legal requirements:

  • Account Data: Retained while your workspace or service connection remains active.
  • Message & Transient Logs: Automatically purged on a rolling retention schedule.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your personal data, phone number, and conversation history at any time.

To request complete deletion of your data, email us at support@blackholeinfiverse.com or bh@blackholeinfiverse.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process and confirm deletion within 30 days.

6. Security of Your Information

We implement technical and organizational security measures, including 256-bit encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3), hashed secrets storage, role-based access control (RBAC), and automated vulnerability monitoring to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.
